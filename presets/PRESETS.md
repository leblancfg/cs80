## Sources and gaps

Sources fetched: Wikipedia CS-80, Gordon Reid CS-80 page, SOS July 1995 CS80 review, Hollow Sun CS-80 page, VintageSynth Explorer, Synthtopia Blade Runner brass article, Reverb Machine Tears in Rain article, Syntorial Blade Runner brass recipe, Arturia Vangelis Tribute page (name only). Source URLs per preset are in presets.json.

Gaps:

- No source reachable in this run gave numeric panel settings (slider positions) for any preset. web_search was largely unavailable, and manualslib, cs80.com setting sheets and scans were not reached. Documented material covers architecture, behaviour and preset names (Brass 1, Brass 2, Flute, Bass, Strings per SOS 1995).
- The full list of the 22 factory preset names was not found; only the few names cited are labelled factory. The rest are original recreation targets named after recordings or sound families.
- The count of fully documented entries is zero; partially documented entries have qualitative documentation only. All numbers are normalized 0..1 estimates, not measurements.
- Attributions to verify: Africa (Toto) brass, Dune, Mutiny on the Bounty, Doctor Who ring mod scream and the Blade Runner harp and rocket noise come from the cited pages and are not confirmed as CS-80 parts. Wonderful Christmastime is not included (Prophet).
- Arturia and GForce preset data was not copied; only the Vangelis Tribute page's existence was used as inspiration.
- Next step: obtain the Yamaha CS-80 owner's manual and setting-sheet scans to fill the 22 factory voices and Vangelis charts.
